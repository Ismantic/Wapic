# Data Licensing

`pattern.txt` is part of Wapic and is covered by the repository's MIT license.

`PeopleDaily1998.zip` is the PFR People's Daily 1998 annotated corpus produced
by the Institute of Computational Linguistics at Peking University and Fujitsu
Research and Development Center. It is included for research reproduction and
is **not** covered by Wapic's MIT license. Its original statement is stored as
`199801/shengming.doc` inside the archive. Users are responsible for complying
with the corpus terms and the rights of the original authors and publisher.

Downloaded Hugging Face models, datasets, and generated training artifacts are
ignored by Git. Their provenance and licensing are documented in their
respective model and dataset cards.
